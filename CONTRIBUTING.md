# Contributing

Suggest a correction or useful imposition workflow through an issue or pull request. Include the official product URL, the exact claim being changed and the date checked. For availability, link the current download or activation page rather than relying on a navigation label.

Describe who the tool fits, access and export restrictions, and a concrete reason to include it. Distinguish documented features from results you personally tested; describe the source file, settings, version and observed outcome for a test. Do not submit copied reviews, fabricated testimonials, unsupported awards or promotional scores.

Disclose a commercial relationship when recommending a product. Featured order is editorial and can change when evidence or availability changes. Keep sources beside material claims and update `data/tools.json` with matching factual changes. The examples are acceptance specifications, not universal machine settings.
